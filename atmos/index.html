Guide

1Search. Hover the circle in the top-left corner to open the bar, then type a name — oxygen, caffeine — or a formula like H2O. Click the circle to keep the bar open.
2Browse. With the box empty the panel lists Elements and Compounds to pick from, plus your last five lookups.
3Orbit. Drag anywhere to turn the structure, scroll to zoom. Let go and it drifts back into a slow spin on its own.
4Read. The card on the right holds the colour key for what you're looking at, plus geometry, bond angles and intermolecular forces.
5Resize. On that card, the outward arrows open the full breakdown and the chevron collapses it to just the name.

About Atmos

Search any element to watch its nucleus and electron shells assemble, or any compound to explore its real 3D geometry — bond angles, hybridisation and intermolecular forces included.

Over 100 million elements and compunds are rendered locally following all quantum rules

website by akshant

How it works

1Fetch. Structures come from PubChem's public chemistry API — over 100 million real compounds.
2Compile. Raw atomic coordinates and bond tables are parsed, then bond angles and hybridisation are measured directly from the geometry.
3Render. The result is rebuilt as a live 3D scene you can orbit, zoom and inspect in real time.

Elements are generated locally from quantum rules — Aufbau order, Pauli exclusion and Hund's rule — rather than fetched.
